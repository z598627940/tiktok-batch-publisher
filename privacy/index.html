Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

This policy describes how dazzlejason (“we”) handles information in TikTok Batch Publisher. Contact: mlcpzhang@gmail.com.

1. Current desktop version

The current Software stores task records, video file paths, account labels/usernames entered by users, captions, hashtags, notes, schedules, and statuses in a local database on the user's device. It reads selected local video files to provide previews and task management. The current version does not connect to TikTok's Content Posting API or automatically upload video content.

2. Information you provide when contacting us

If you contact our support email, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and message, and use it to respond.

3. Future TikTok integration

We plan to offer optional TikTok account authorization and content posting features. Before enabling these features, we will update this policy to explain exactly which TikTok account data, authorization credentials, and publishing records are collected, transmitted, stored, retained, or shared, and how users can disconnect their accounts and request deletion. No such integration is active in the current version.

4. Sharing and third parties

We do not currently operate a server that receives the local task database or media files from the desktop Software. If you choose to visit third-party services such as TikTok, GitHub Pages, or linked websites, their own policies apply.

5. Storage and security

Current task data resides on the user's computer. You control local files and backups and can delete the local application data from your device. No system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and deletion

Local task data remains on your device until you remove it. Support correspondence may be retained as needed to respond and maintain records; you can request deletion by contacting us, subject to applicable obligations.

7. Children

The Software is intended for creators who are eligible to use TikTok under its terms and is not directed at children.

8. Your rights and contact

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information. For privacy questions or requests, contact mlcpzhang@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when our practices or features change, including before activating TikTok API integrations.
